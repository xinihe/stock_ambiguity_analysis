\section*{Response to Editor Comments}

We sincerely thank the Editor for the provisional acceptance of our paper and for the constructive guidance regarding the final manuscript preparation for Finance Research Letters (FRL). We are thrilled to proceed to the publication stage. 

We have carefully reviewed all the final checks and formatting requirements outlined in your email. Below we provide a point-by-point summary of the actions taken to ensure the manuscript fully complies with the journal's standards.

\subsection*{Comment 1: Formatting, Copy Editing, and Font Color}
\textit{"This is a provisional acceptance of your paper subject to a careful final check of formatting and copy editing... and that the document has the same font and font colour (black) throughout- ie no use of highlighted text"}

\begin{highlightbox}
\textbf{Response:} We have conducted a thorough final proofread of the manuscript to ensure grammatical correctness and stylistic consistency. In accordance with the guidelines, we have removed all colored text, tracked changes, and highlights from previous revision rounds. The entire document now uses a standard black font throughout.
\end{highlightbox}

\subsection*{Comment 2: Literature, Conclusion, and Implications}
\textit{"Please also ensure that you have referenced key recent papers in finance in the introduction and explain how you build on these in the conclusion. Ideally include one or two sentences on implications for future research and policy in the conclusion section."}

\begin{highlightbox}
\textbf{Response:} We have carefully reviewed the Introduction to ensure that key recent papers in the finance literature are appropriately cited and discussed. Furthermore, we expanded the Conclusion to explicitly connect our findings back to this recent literature. 

As requested, we have also added specific sentences in the Conclusion outlining the implications of our study for both future academic research and practical policy design.
\end{highlightbox}

\begin{highlightbox}
\textbf{Revisions in the Manuscript:}

In the Introduction, we added the following text to clarify our contribution and cite recent literature:
\vspace{0.2cm}
\begin{quote}
\itshape "Recent literature has increasingly highlighted the role of geopolitical shocks in asset pricing (e.g., Zhang et al., 2024). However, how these shocks translate into ambiguity, specifically within emerging markets, remains underexplored... Building on the theoretical distinction established above, our framework shows that GPR significantly increases both ambiguity and volatility. Furthermore, ambiguity operates alongside volatility to explain GPR's effect on returns, illustrating the complementary nature of these two uncertainty channels."
\end{quote}

In the Conclusion, we added the following text to build on these recent papers and state implications for future research and policy:
\vspace{0.2cm}
\begin{quote}
\itshape "Building upon recent insights that underscore the broad asset-pricing implications of geopolitical shocks, our study demonstrates that these shocks transmit to emerging markets not merely through volatility but through the distinct channel of ambiguity. For policymakers, our results suggest that mitigating geopolitical fallout requires more than standard liquidity provision; it necessitates enhanced institutional transparency and targeted support for non-SOEs to directly alleviate model uncertainty. Future research should extend this cross-entropy-based ambiguity framework to other asset classes and explore how the ambiguity premium fluctuates across different geopolitical regimes, ranging from localized trade disputes to global military conflicts."
\end{quote}
\end{highlightbox}
